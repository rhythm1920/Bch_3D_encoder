3-D BCH encoder · N = 15, T = 2 · GF(16), x⁴+x+1

Three encoder architectures, after place and route

Each design maps 3,163 message bits to a 3,375-bit (15×15×15) codeword. Designs 1 and 2 were run on Virtex-7 xc7v2000t-1, both in full and as a 225-point slice (the output plane i''=0). Design 3 was run in full on Virtex-7 and on Artix-7 xc7a200t-1. Tool: Vivado 2026.1, out of context, flatten rebuilt, no phys_opt. Updated 31 Oct 2026.

Design 1 · baseline

Each of the 3,375 output points is computed on its own. Point p XORs, for every class k, the sub-message bits selected by the trace mask of Tr(Sk·αe). That is about 1,716 inputs per point, and nothing is shared between points.

  • Slice: 37,882 LUTs, about 119 MHz, 3.03 W
  • Full design: about 568k LUTs (slice ×15, estimate)
  • The full design could not be synthesised (out of memory at 10.2 GB)
Design 2 · shared multiplier bank

Each product Tr(Sk·αe) is computed once. That gives 11,500 wires in the full design and 10,608 in the slice. Every point then XORs exactly 798 bank wires, one per class.

  • Slice: 12,816 LUTs, about 130 MHz, 1.50 W
  • Full design: 181,728 LUTs after synthesis
  • The full design did not route: 25,832 nets unrouted, 99.8% congestion
Design 3 · time-multiplexed

The same bank feeds only 225 trees, one per (i, i'). Each cycle the Sk registers are multiplied by α−j3, so cycle c produces the plane i''=c. A codeword takes 15 cycles.

  • Virtex-7: 17,196 LUTs, 166.7 MHz, 3.35 W
  • Artix-7: 17,209 LUTs, 117.6 MHz, 1.92 W
  • The only full encoder that routes

What the numbers say

Sharing the multipliers is worth 3× in area. In the plane-sized slices, design 2 needs 12,816 LUTs against design 1's 37,882, and 0.85 W of dynamic power against 2.36 W. A design 1 point XORs about 1,716 raw sub-message bits. A design 2 point XORs 798 precomputed products, each a 1–4 bit parity built once in the bank.

The full parallel encoders fail on wiring, not logic. Fifteen design 2 slices would be about 192k LUTs. That matches the full synthesis (181,728) and is only 15% of the device. But all 3,375 trees read from the same bank, so routing demand grows with points × classes. Routing peaked at 99.8% congestion and left 66,912 node overlaps.

Time-multiplexing is a design 2 slice used 15 times. Design 3 has the slice's bank and 225 trees. Rotating S makes those trees compute plane c on cycle c. It costs 15× in throughput, but it is the only full encoder that routes, and on Artix-7 it uses just 13% of an xc7a200t.
